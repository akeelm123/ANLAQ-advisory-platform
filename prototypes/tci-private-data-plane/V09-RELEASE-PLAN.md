1. Versioned fictional case inputs linked to the authorised Aurora and calibration workbooks, with synthetic provenance and explicit limits.
2. Evidence collection and independent screening, exact extraction citations for each dimension, separate stakeholder accounts and material contradictions.
3. Human-led evidence validation for 28 criteria, including adverse, insufficient and superseded evidence; no invented findings.
4. Restricted scoring only when all current required criteria have independently accepted anchors. Compare arithmetic to source fixtures without forcing incomplete evidence into a score.
5. Reviewed executive brief, explicitly chosen owner direction and accountable conditions/actions; no automated investment recommendation.
6. Accepted baseline and follow-up snapshots, comparable periods, action status, distinction between condition change and evidence support change.
7. Automated regression and integrated scenario tests, browser demonstration and acceptance report committed to prototyping.
8. Private pilot operational readiness checklist stating unresolved identity/access, retention, audit, data protection and approval gates. No deployment or real-client handling.

## Acceptance gates
