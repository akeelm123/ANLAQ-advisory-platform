## Authority
Owner accepted v0.6 on 2026-11-27. V06-APPROVAL.json pins the tested candidate and acceptance record. V07-RELEASE-PLAN.md scopes the synthetic comparative candidate. No real-client data, production scores, automatic recommendations or deployment.

## Delivered
- Immutable encrypted point-in-time snapshots preserve criterion evidence, source IDs, quotes, locators, digest, methodology, scope, request gaps, interview contradictions, reviewed executive actions/direction and optional pilot score records. The state digest provides a freshness check, not a proof of source truth. Snapshot metadata includes explicit calendar period and measurement basis.
- Independent reviewer verifies period, basis, evidence and reconciliation references. Draft becomes stale when underlying state changes; accepted history remains available even if later evidence is superseded.
- Comparison restricted to accepted snapshots within one assessment, checked equal scope and methodology and matching stated population/measurement basis; periods must be strictly ordered and non-overlapping. Both assessor and independent reviewer explicitly confirm comparability; a text match alone is not semantic assurance.
- Seven dimension summaries and 28 criterion transitions explicitly distinguish met/adverse condition movement, unchanged supported condition, support gained, support lost and unresolved both. Evidence loss is not a performance decline. Two complete, independently reviewed synthetic pilot scores are required for exact provisional score delta; no partial-score imputation, no reweighting, no confidence bands or automated decisions.
- Every baseline reviewed executive action is accounted for with status, accountable owner, rationale and follow-up finding IDs. Claimed closure requires supported follow-up evidence and independent review. Evidence links do not themselves certify that an action is effective.
- Owners see historical accepted snapshots and reviewed comparisons; contributors cannot see them. Every record is immutable, encrypted, scoped and audited. Production release remains locked.

## Limitations and remaining gates
The browser demonstrates an incomplete fictional single-criterion baseline, not a fully assessed engagement. Complete score delta and action closure cases are structural in-process tests. Period and measurement-basis declarations require human semantic review; matching strings do not prove populations or metrics match. Comparison is within one assessment (cross-assessment/cross-tenant comparisons intentionally unsupported). Period snapshots preserve current evidence at capture, not reconstructed past evidence. No automated causal attribution, real-world semantic calibration, client-ready output, production security, real-client processing or public release. Prior optional model-specific browser tests were not rerun; earlier calibration and scoring thresholds/rounding/N/A gates remain unresolved. Independent owner acceptance of v0.7 is required before an integrated Aurora synthetic pilot release.
