# TCI v0.6 — Executive decision support
Version 0.1 · 2026-11-27 · Planned, owner acceptance pending

## Objective
Translate independently accepted evidence-supported conditions into a traceable human-owned executive decision brief. Do not derive a decision from pilot numbers, evidence class, thresholds or a model output.

## Acceptance work
1. Show current validated findings, limitations, owners, sources, contradictions and unresolved requests. Distinguish adverse evidence from insufficient evidence.
2. Permit an assessor to draft decision options, rationale, consequences, explicit material conditions and follow-up owners; no automatic option selection.
3. Require independent review of every linked current finding, evidence and material gap before marking a draft reviewed. Preserve prior versions and revoke current readiness when supporting evidence changes.
4. Keep pilot scores separate and clearly provisional; do not infer a confidence band, risk cap or investment decision. Withhold otherwise unsupported conclusions.
5. Show separate human-owned decision direction (Invest / Correct / Reset / Stop / Reassess), rationale and residual conditions; keep production release locked.
6. Test synthetic complete/incomplete evidence, adverse findings, open and resolved contradictions, source supersession, role isolation, independent review and denied release. Demonstrate a fictional Aurora scenario in the browser. Commit code, acceptance report and test evidence to prototyping only.

## Gates
No client data, live deployment, production scoring or automated decisions. v0.5 calibration and rounding issues remain open. v0.6 candidate requires separate owner acceptance.
