## Implemented behavior

Ten approved preparation trigger types drive adaptive proposals based on explicit assessor observations, mapped current evidence, open contradictions and reviewer returns. A human chooses/edits and records the question. Optional local model wording is constrained to the already-selected trigger and exact source response quote, checked for schema and quote integrity, recorded with model digest and encrypted payload; it cannot select a different trigger or send any message. The approved rule remains available if model output fails. Human-reviewed model wording is linked to the follow-up. Verbatim response and interpretation are separate records. Reviewer confirmation is confirmation of capture, not validation of a business claim or evidence sufficiency; open contradictions remain open for independent reconciliation.

All seven planned synthetic two-persona golden cases were executed at service level with assessor-specified observations and explicit conflict recording. This is not automatic semantic conflict detection. Actual local wording benchmark initially returned six valid proposals and rejected one schema failure; a bounded retry was implemented and all seven cases returned structurally valid proposals in the retest. Generated wording still needs human quality review; for example, training participation must not be treated as observed adoption. No production interview accuracy claim.

## Go/no-go

Go: formative synthetic review and further v0.3 refinement. No-go: live interviews/recording, automatic stakeholder contact, automatic semantic validation or contradiction resolution, scoring, real-client processing or production deployment. Formal v0.3 acceptance requires owner review of question/follow-up usefulness, additional independent semantic cases and a review decision. Private pilot hosting, identity, retention, processor approvals and security acceptance remain separate.
