## Before beginning
The project owner identifies separate named contributor, assessor, reviewer and owner participants, confirms no real records, and records machine and backup custody assurance privately. The supplied files in uat-fixtures are labelled mock evidence, not Aurora source documents. Do not put the actual vault, passwords, human names, device-security report or pilot evidence in GitHub. Use the persistent local launcher and a dedicated synthetic vault (not any real assessment). Keep separate roles, no password sharing. The model and pilot scoring are disabled in persistent mode.

## Human acceptance record (store completed record privately)
For each U01–U13 record tester role, execution date, expected result, observed outcome (PASS/FAIL/NOT RUN), issue ID/severity, evidence reference and independently reviewed disposition. Name participants/custody privately. No auto-completion or implied consent. Owner signs go/no-go only when required machine, scope, security, privacy, retention and calibration gates are separately accepted.

## Assessor calibration
Independent assessors use the same fictional source pack without sharing ratings; compare criterion condition, evidence class/strength, citation appropriateness and missing/contradictory evidence decisions. Document reasons for disagreement and reconcile against approved anchors. No scores in the persistent mode and no automatic judgement. Formal real/historical-data calibration, Atlas rounding, risk/cap thresholds and N/A treatment remain pending.
