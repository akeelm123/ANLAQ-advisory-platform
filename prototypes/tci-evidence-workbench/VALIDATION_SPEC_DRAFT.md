# Evidence validation specification — v0.1 draft, not approved
26 November 2026

Basis: local TCI Evidence Classification Standard and Seven-Dimension Evidence Guide, Response Bank Pilot and Assessment Methodology. The reconstructed Master Evidence Register v1.0-reconstructed-rc.1 is approved as the working baseline. Final criterion-specific Evidence Validation Specification approval remains separate.

Cross-criterion prototype controls: require a source title, owner, version, reporting period, source locator and locally computed SHA-256 digest. A hash identifies bytes; it does not prove authenticity or accuracy. Record the claim separately from evidence, E1 documented / E2 corroborated / E3 asserted / E4 unknown, the gap, contradictions and follow-up. E2 requires separate source bytes and owners as a preliminary structural check; assessor must verify actual independence. An open contradiction prevents acceptance, even for E1/E2. E3/E4 remain draft or returned. An accepted evidence review is not a validated executive judgement. A named different reviewer and explicit acknowledgement are required; identity is not authenticated in this local alpha.

Per-criterion specification still required for all 28 criteria: approved criterion ID and intent, mandatory/conditional/supplementary evidence IDs, exact extraction fields, validation tests, independence rules, contradiction procedure, sufficiency and scoring eligibility, follow-up and output template. Use the approved workbook mapping (89 relationships reported in prior conversation) rather than inventing mappings. The local source-derived 28 questions are available in sources.json for review only.

All numerical scoring, executive release and public deployment remain disabled regardless of review.
